About

CSE graduate, Dhaka International University (2022–2026).

I like problems that need both careful engineering and careful thinking: shipping an app people can use, and testing whether a model is right for the right reasons.

On the software side I work in C++, Java, Python and JavaScript, and build full web apps with React, Node.js and Tailwind. I sharpened my fundamentals through competitive programming on Codeforces, LeetCode and HackerRank.

On the research side I focus on deep learning, computer vision and explainable AI. My thesis and manuscripts look at shortcut learning, data quality and failure-aware prediction in plant disease diagnosis.

At university I often led project groups, which taught me to plan, split work and communicate clearly.
